Refund Policy

We want you to shop with confidence. This Refund Policy explains the conditions for returns, exchanges, cancellations, and refunds for purchases made through banshiwale.

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Overview

At banshiwale, customer satisfaction is our priority. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we offer a fair return and refund process subject to the conditions outlined in this policy.

2. Eligibility for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following conditions:

  • Return request is raised within the eligible return period.
  • Product is unused, unworn, and in its original condition.
  • Original packaging, invoice, and tags are intact.
  • Product has not been damaged after delivery.

3. Non-Returnable Items

Certain products cannot be returned or refunded due to hygiene, customization, or product nature.

  • Customized or personalized jewellery.
  • Products damaged due to misuse.
  • Items without original packaging or invoice.
  • Gift cards or promotional items, where applicable.

4. Return Request Process

To initiate a return, contact our customer support team with your order number and the reason for the return. Once your request is reviewed, we'll provide the next steps for returning the product.

5. Inspection & Approval

Once we receive your returned product, it will undergo a quality inspection to verify that it meets our return eligibility criteria.

If the returned item satisfies our inspection requirements, your return request will be approved. If it does not meet the eligibility conditions, the product may be returned to you without a refund.

6. Refund Process

Once your return has been approved, your refund will be initiated using the original payment method wherever possible.

Shipping charges, gift wrapping fees, taxes, or any additional service charges may not be refundable unless required by applicable law or if the return is due to an error on our part.

7. Exchanges

Eligible products may be exchanged for another size, design, or available product, subject to stock availability and our exchange policy.

If the requested replacement product is unavailable, we may offer an alternative product or process a refund, depending on the circumstances.

8. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please notify us as soon as possible after delivery.

  • Provide your order number.
  • Share clear photographs of the product and packaging.
  • Contact our customer support before using the item.

After verification, we will arrange a replacement, exchange, or refund, depending on the situation.

9. Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before they have been shipped. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible and the order will be subject to our return policy instead.

banshiwale reserves the right to cancel any order in cases of suspected fraud, pricing errors, payment failures, stock unavailability, or other exceptional circumstances. If payment has already been received, the applicable refund will be initiated.

10. Refund Timelines

Once your refund has been approved, we will initiate the refund promptly. The time required for the amount to reflect in your account depends on your payment provider and banking institution.

  • Refunds are processed after successful inspection of the returned product.
  • Processing times may vary depending on your payment method.
  • Your bank or payment provider may require additional time to credit the amount.
