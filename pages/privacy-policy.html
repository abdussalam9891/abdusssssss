Privacy Policy

We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how banshiwale collects, uses, stores, and safeguards your data when you browse or shop on our website.

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or make a purchase from banshiwale, we may collect information that helps us process your orders, improve your shopping experience, and communicate with you effectively.

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Shipping Address
  • Billing Address
  • Order History
  • Payment Information (processed securely by third-party payment providers)

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect helps us provide a secure, efficient, and personalized shopping experience.

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Send order confirmations, invoices, and shipping updates.
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized transactions.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Cookies & Analytics

banshiwale uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, improve website performance, understand visitor behavior, and enhance your overall browsing experience.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although certain website features may not function properly.

4. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and never sell your personal information to third parties.

Your information may only be shared with trusted service providers such as payment gateways, logistics partners, technology providers, or government authorities when legally required.

5. Payment Security

All online payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant payment partners. banshiwale does not store your complete debit card, credit card, or UPI credentials on its servers.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

While we strive to maintain the highest security standards, no method of data transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent for certain communications at any time by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

banshiwale does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that such information has been provided, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or website functionality. The latest version will always be available on this page along with its effective date.
